Zikr Deen — Privacy Policy

Draft for the app owner to review before publishing. Update the contact email and date below. Last updated: November 2026.

What stays on your phone

Prayer times are calculated on your phone. Your city, settings, progress, tasbih counts and chat history stay on the device. If you use GPS for prayer times, your location is used only to calculate times and is not sent to us.

What we collect (only if you choose)

  • Guests: an anonymous counter per phone (first/last open dates, number of app opens, number of Ask AI questions). No name, no email.
  • Google login: your name and email from your Google account, the same counters, and — only if you press Backup — your app progress backup. You can restore it on a new phone.
  • Ask AI: the question you type or speak is sent to Google Gemini (directly or through our server) to produce an answer. Question text is not stored by us; only the number of questions is counted.
  • WhatsApp (optional): if you link WhatsApp, your phone number and learning progress are handled by the WhatsApp bot you chose to connect.

Permissions

Notifications and alarms (azan reminders), location (optional, prayer times and Qibla), camera (optional, live Qibla/AR view), microphone (optional, voice questions). Each can be denied; the related feature then stays off.

What we do not do

No ads. No selling of data. No tracking beyond the counters above.

Delete your data

In the app: More > Account > Delete my account removes your cloud data. Clearing the app's storage removes everything on your phone. You can also write to us at the email below.

Religious content

Ask AI answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. They are not a fatwa. For important rulings ask a qualified scholar.

Children

The app is for general learning. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the optional classroom features chosen by a teacher.
